You keep the code. Every tier hands over the agents, prompts, Supabase schema and
Make/n8n scenarios to your GitHub and your accounts. No handoff fee, no export

Every engagement opens with a free 60-minute AI GTM audit and a live demo on your own

## Sprint guarantee

If the engine books fewer than four ICP-matched held meetings in its first 30 days
live, the first month of GTM Partnership is free. Three conditions: you have supplied
the ICP, a working sending domain set is in place, and one named person owns replies.
Meetings are counted by the CRM Auto-Pilot log. Once per company.

A held meeting is one the prospect attended, matched to your ICP, and logged in your
CRM by the engine.

## Switching from an agency retainer

A Sprint builds the engine inside your accounts for $3,000 while an existing contract
runs down. Inside a term with another vendor, AI Ropeway credits up to $1,000 of their
setup fee against Partnership month one, on proof of contract.
